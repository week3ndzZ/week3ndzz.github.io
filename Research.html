Research

Research Interests

I study causal and intervention-aware representations for reliable prediction under distribution shift, with an emphasis on biological systems and the internal mechanisms of modern AI models.

  • Causal Representation Learning: identifying stable latent mechanisms from complex observational and interventional data.
  • AI for Genomics: single-cell perturbation modeling, cellular dynamics, and out-of-distribution response prediction.
  • AI Safety: understanding and steering internal model representations for reliable behavior.
