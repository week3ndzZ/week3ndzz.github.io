About Me

I am a PhD candidate at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML), Adelaide University, where I am a member of the Causal AI Group. I am principally supervised by Prof. Javen Qinfeng Shi, with Dr. Yuhang Liu as co-supervisor. Before commencing my PhD, I received an M.S. in Computer Technology (2024) from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, based at the Computer Network Information Center, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and a B.S. in Computer Science and Technology (2021) from Hefei University of Technology.

Research Interests

My research studies how causal structure can make learning systems more reliable, interpretable, and useful for scientific discovery. I am particularly interested in learning intervention-aware representations from complex biological data and predicting how cellular systems respond beyond the conditions observed during training. I also investigate the internal representations that shape the reliability and safety of modern AI models.

  • Causal Representation Learning: learning stable latent mechanisms from observational and interventional data.
  • AI for Genomics: modeling cellular programs and predicting responses to unseen genetic perturbations.
  • AI Safety: understanding and steering internal representations for reliable model behavior.
