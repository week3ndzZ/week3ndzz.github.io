01 / About

Building AI that reasons
about cause and effect.

I am a PhD student at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML), Adelaide University, and a member of the Causal AI Group. I am supervised by Prof. Javen Qinfeng Shi, with Dr. Yuhang Liu as co-supervisor.

My work sits at the intersection of causal representation learning and AI for science. I am especially interested in learning intervention-aware representations from complex biological data, with the goal of predicting how systems respond beyond the conditions observed during training. I also explore the internal representations that shape the reliability and safety of modern AI models.
